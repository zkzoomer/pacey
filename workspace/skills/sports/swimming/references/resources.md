## Broken links and maintenance

If a link fails, search the exact title on the same organization's domain. Use the official replacement where possible and record the retrieval date. Do not silently substitute a forum answer or commercial summary for an original study or rulebook.

If a page requires membership or its video is inaccessible, say so and use accessible text or another verified demonstration. Never claim to have watched a video merely because its thumbnail or title was retrieved.

Review rules before each competition-specific answer. Review training sources when a substantive contradiction arises or the skill is being revised; do not browse every stable basic cue on every turn. Preserve the distinction between an update to this general skill and an update to one athlete's personal profile.
